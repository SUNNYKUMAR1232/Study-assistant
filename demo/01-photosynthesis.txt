Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose. It happens in two stages. The light-dependent reactions occur in the thylakoid membranes, splitting water to release oxygen and producing ATP and NADPH. The Calvin cycle occurs in the stroma and uses that ATP and NADPH to fix carbon dioxide into glucose via the enzyme RuBisCO. The overall equation is 6CO2 + 6H2O + light -> C6H12O6 + 6O2. Chlorophyll a absorbs light most strongly in the blue-violet and red regions of the spectrum, which is why leaves appear green: green light is reflected rather than absorbed.
