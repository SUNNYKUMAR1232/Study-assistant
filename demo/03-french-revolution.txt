The French Revolution began in 1789 when the Estates-General was convened for the first time since 1614. The Third Estate, representing the common people, declared itself the National Assembly in June 1789 and swore the Tennis Court Oath, pledging not to disband until France had a constitution. The Bastille was stormed on 14 July 1789, an event now marked as the national holiday. The Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen was adopted in August 1789. The monarchy was abolished in September 1792, and Louis XVI was executed in January 1793. The Reign of Terror, directed by the Committee of Public Safety under Robespierre, lasted from 1793 to 1794 and ended with Robespierre's own execution in July 1794.
